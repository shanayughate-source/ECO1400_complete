INTERPRETATION NOTES
1. Lower mean absolute alpha and lower alpha RMSE indicate smaller in-sample pricing errors.
2. A GRS p-value below the chosen significance level rejects that model's null that all 25 alphas are zero.
3. Do not rank models by comparing GRS F-statistics or p-values mechanically; each is a separate joint pricing test and the models have different factor counts.
4. R-squared is descriptive and should not be the sole model-selection criterion.
5. FF3 and FF5 are official published specifications, but they are not strictly nested because their SMB factors differ in construction.
6. Coefficient differences for SMB should be treated cautiously. FF3 SMB is based on Size/B-M portfolios, while FF5 SMB averages Size/B-M, Size/OP and Size/Investment size premiums.
7. Factor-input diagnostics report differences in Mkt-RF, HML and RF across the two official input files.
8. Alpha differences in the portfolio table are descriptive; this script does not calculate a formal test of the difference between estimated alphas.
